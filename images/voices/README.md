# Author photos

Drop a `.jpg` per voice here and its grid square on voices.html shows it
automatically, with a duotone treatment (grayscale + desk-color wash) that
makes photos from different sources look consistent. Square-ish crops work
best. No photo → the tile shows the voice's shape mark and initials.

Wikimedia Commons is a good source of freely licensed portraits — check
each file's license and attribution requirements.
