🗂 Arxiu

"Save to Arxiu" sends each essay to your voices Apps Script, which builds a PDF named LastName - Year - Title in the Drive folder "Arxiu — AI Voices". To get the URL: Apps Script editor → Deploy → New deployment → Web app (Execute as: Me · Access: Anyone) → copy the /exec URL. See the README.

For paywalled articles (FT, NYT, Economist…): drag this button to your bookmarks bar, then click it while reading any article. It sends the full text as your logged-in browser sees it to the same PDF pipeline and confirms with "Saved to Arxiu ✓". (Browsers give bookmarklets a generic globe icon — the 🗂 in the name is its marker; you can rename it after dragging.)
